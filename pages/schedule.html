Workshop

The goal of this workshop is to engage in a detailed discussion on multimodal retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), with a special focus on the standout solutions from the KDD Cup 2025 on Comprehensive RAG for Multi-Modal, Multi-Turn Challenge. The workshop is an in-person event co-located with KDD 2025 in Toronto, Canada on Monday, August 04, 2025. See paper submission, registration, hotel, and venue details for attending.

The event will run from 08:00 to 12:00, with a coffee break (and poster session) scheduled from 09:30 to 10:00.
