Workshop

The goal of this workshop is to engage in a detailed discussion on multimodal retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), with a special focus on the standout solutions from the KDD Cup 2025 on Comprehensive RAG for Multi-Modal, Multi-Turn Challenge. The workshop is an in-person event co-located with KDD 2025 in Toronto, Canada on Monday, August 04, 2025. See paper submission, registration, hotel, and venue details for attending.

The event will run from 08:00 to 12:00, with a coffee break (and poster session) scheduled from 09:30 to 10:00.

Location: Toronto Convention Centre, 712.

08:00–09:30 Session 1
  • [08:00-08:30] Opening remarks and competition summary
  • [08:30-09:00] Participation teams - talk 1-2 (15 mins each)
  • [09:00-9:30] Keynote talk 1
    • Jianwei Yang: Toward General-Purpose Multimodal Agents: From GUIs to Robots and Beyond
09:30-10:00 Coffee break & poster session
10:00-11:55 Session 2
  • [10:00-10:35] Keynote talk 2
    • Jingrui He: Towards Multimodal Understanding on Rich Data IID vs. Non-IID
  • [10:35–11:05] Participation teams - talk 3-4 (15 min each)
  • [11:05-11:35] Keynote talk 3
    • Dhruv Batra: Scouts: multi-modal agentic search for monitoring the web
  • [11:35–11:55] Participation teams - spotlight talks (5 min each)
11:55–12:00 Closing remarks
